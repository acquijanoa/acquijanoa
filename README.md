## Hola 👋

I am Álvaro Quijano-Angarita (he/him), a PhD candidate in Biostatistics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC), advised by Dr. Michael R. Kosorok and Dr. Kevin J. Anstrom. I earned my master’s degree in Biostatistics at UNC on 2023, supported by a Fulbright Scholarship. My research focuses on the intersection of complex survey data methods and Bayesian inference approaches to obtain population-representative health outcomes and improve missing-data imputation using observational data, with applications in public health.
