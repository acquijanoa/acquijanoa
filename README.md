## Hola 👋

I am Álvaro Quijano-Angarita (he/him), a PhD candidate in Biostatistics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Dr. Michael R. Kosorok and Dr. Kevin J. Anstrom. I earned my master’s degree in Biostatistics from the same university in 2023, supported by a Fulbright Scholarship. My research focuses on the intersection of complex survey data methods, robust Bayesian inference, and kernel-based approaches to obtain population-representative health outcomes and improve missing-data imputation using observational data, with applications to causal inference in public health research.
